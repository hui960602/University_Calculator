How it works

Semester grade = sum of (score × weight) for every item.

The points you already have are added up first. The gap to your target is then divided by the weight that's left, which gives the average score you need on the remaining items.

If more than one item is left, the result is the average you need across them. Bonus points or curves from the teacher are not included.
