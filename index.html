Why this site

College life comes with a lot of small calculations: who owes whom after dinner, what my GPA will be, what I need on the final, and how many credits are left before graduation.

Each of these usually means a calculator app, a spreadsheet, or a messy note. University Calculator puts them in one place, with the rules already built in.
