General Education (10 credits)

2 credits from each of the four categories, plus 2 interdisciplinary credits that must also come from the four categories. All courses must be taught in English. Credits above 10 are not counted.

Electives (43 credits)

Of the 43 elective credits: at least 18 from this program, at least 12 from the College of Informatics, and at most 13 free electives. Program electives are listed below; add courses from other departments under "Other electives".

Other electives

Courses from other departments in the College of Informatics count as college electives. Courses from other colleges count as free electives.
